3. Fair Play and Anti-Cheat

Cheating, hacking, exploitation, and any other conduct that undermines a fair gameplay environment are prohibited.

Prohibited conduct includes, but is not limited to: using hacked clients, X-ray, combat/movement/automation cheats, or other unauthorized third-party software; modifying game files, network traffic, or server communications to gain an unfair advantage or evade detection; exploiting bugs, duplication glitches, or vulnerabilities in the server, plugins, or game mechanics; bypassing or disabling anti-cheat, verification, or ban systems; and creating, distributing, or promoting cheats intended for use on this server.

Conduct may be treated as a violation regardless of whether the attempt succeeded, was discovered, or caused actual damage.

4. Player Conduct and Community Order

Players must respect other players and staff, and must not engage in conduct that harms server security, community order, or the normal gameplay experience of others.

Prohibited conduct includes, but is not limited to: harassing, threatening, insulting, or discriminating against another player; posting hateful, explicit, illegal, or severely disturbing content; disclosing or threatening to disclose another person's private information; impersonating khoichon, staff, or another player; spam, malicious advertisements, or phishing links; and maliciously fabricating reports or evidence against another player.

Refusal to cooperate with reasonable staff requests made while investigating a violation or maintaining server order is itself a violation.

5. Investigations and Evidence

To investigate violations, protect players, and maintain server security, staff may reasonably collect, retain, and review information related to the server, including chat logs, server and plugin logs, login and connection records, screenshots, recordings, and evidence submitted by players or staff.

Staff may determine whether a violation occurred based on technical detection results, logs, behavioral patterns, reports, and player statements.

To protect detection methods, reporter privacy, and server security, staff are not required to disclose all evidence, internal logs, or the identity of a reporter to a punished player.

6. Enforcement and Punishments

Punishments depend on the severity and circumstances of the violation and may include a warning, removal of illegitimate items or advantages, restoration or rollback of damaged areas, temporary restrictions, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban, as described in the Server Rules.

Any confirmed form of cheating, serious griefing, repeated violations, attacks against the server, or ban evasion may result in an immediate ban without a prior warning.

Staff may impose enforcement action without prior notice or an opportunity to correct the conduct, though minor first-time issues will typically be addressed with a warning at staff's discretion.

7. Ban Evasion and Alternate Accounts

After being banned or restricted, a player must not evade, bypass, or weaken the effect of that action by any means, including creating or using another account, borrowing or using another person's account, or asking another person to access the server on their behalf.

Ban evasion is a separate violation. Associated accounts may be banned, and an existing temporary ban may be extended or converted into a permanent ban.

Alternate accounts may not be used to bypass restrictions, gain an unfair advantage, or evade server limits, even absent an existing ban.

8. Ban Appeals

If you believe a ban was issued in error, you may submit an appeal through the official Discord published by Khoi's Minecraft Server.

An appeal must be submitted within fourteen calendar days from the date you first see the ban notice, and should include your account/player name, an explanation of the relevant events, and any supporting evidence.

Appeals submitted after the deadline, containing false information, or submitted by an unrelated third party may be rejected without further review. Submitting an appeal does not guarantee a ban will be removed and does not automatically suspend enforcement.
